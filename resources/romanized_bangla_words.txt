# Romanized (Latin-script) Bangla words used to tell "romanized" reviews apart
# from English ones. One lowercase word per line; "#"
# starts a comment.
#
# The plan suggests starting from 50-100 common words. Romanized Bangla has no
# standard spelling, so each word is listed with the variants actually seen in
# Play reviews (e.g. hoina / hoyna, parteci / partesi / partechi), which is why
# the list is longer.
#
# Deliberately left out because they are English words or common English
# tokens in these reviews: taka/tk (currency name used in English reviews),
# to, pay, bar, din, mash, tin, niche, sir, problem, number, er, te, age,
# hole, pore, pare, den, rat, ken, r, o, e, a.
#
# After the manual check of 100 reviews (Lang_check_100 sheet), add or remove
# words here, re-run clean_tag.py and draw_samples.py, and record the change in
# the decision log.
